# Tron

## Goal
Two players race light cycles. Do not hit a wall or a trail.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Player 1 (red) |
| W A S D | Player 2 (green) |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. Both bikes leave a solid trail.
2. Walls wrap around the screen.
3. The last player alive wins.

## Tips
- Trap your opponent against your own trail.
