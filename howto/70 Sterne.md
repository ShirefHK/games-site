# Sterne

## Goal
Place stars. Exactly 2 per row, column and region; stars may not touch.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Empty → dot → star |
| R | New puzzle |

## How to play
1. Click a cell to cycle: empty → dot (no star) → star.
2. Exactly 2 stars per row, column and region.
3. Stars may not touch, not even diagonally.

## Tips
- Mark dots around stars to avoid touching.
