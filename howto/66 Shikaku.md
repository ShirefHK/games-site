# Shikaku

## Goal
Divide the grid into rectangles. Each holds exactly one number, equal to its area.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click / drag | Draw a rectangle |
| R | New puzzle |

## How to play
1. Drag out a rectangle around a number.
2. Its area must equal the number.
3. Cover every cell.

## Tips
- Start with big numbers.
