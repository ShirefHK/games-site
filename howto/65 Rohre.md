# Rohre

## Goal
Connect the nodes with pipes. Numbers show pipe ends at that node.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click the gap between nodes | Add pipe |
| Click again | Double pipe, then remove |
| R | New puzzle |

## How to play
1. Click between neighbouring nodes.
2. Numbers = pipe ends at that node.
3. Make one connected pipe system.

## Tips
- Nodes with a high number help.
