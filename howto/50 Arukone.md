# Arukone

## Goal
Connect each pair of letters with a line.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag | Draw from a letter to its partner |
| S | Show solution |
| R | New puzzle |

## How to play
1. Drag from a letter to its partner.
2. Lines cannot cross or share cells.
3. Dragging back erases.

## Tips
- Do the pairs along the edge first.
