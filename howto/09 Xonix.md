# Xonix

## Goal
Cut off and claim areas of the field while avoiding the enemies.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Move |
| Esc | Restart |

## How to play
1. Move out from the safe edge to draw a line across the field.
2. Close the line back to a safe edge to claim the area you enclosed.
3. Do not let an enemy touch your unfinished line.

## Tips
- Take small bites early; keep large cuts for when the enemies are far away.
