# Doodle Jump

## Goal
Keep bouncing upward from platform to platform without falling off the bottom of the screen.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → | Steer left / right |
| R | Restart after a fall |

## How to play
1. The character jumps by itself whenever it lands on a platform.
2. Steer to land on the next platform above.
3. If you fall off the bottom the run ends. Press R to try again.

## Tips
- Aim for the middle of platforms so a small drift does not make you miss.
