# SameGame

## Goal
Pop groups of same-coloured bubbles and clear the board.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Pop a group of 2 or more |
| R | New board |

## How to play
1. Click a group of 2 or more touching bubbles of the same colour.
2. Bigger groups score (n − 2)².
3. Clear the board for a +1000 bonus.

## Tips
- Save big groups for later and avoid stranding single bubbles.
