# Reversi

## Goal
Trap enemy discs between yours to flip them. Most discs wins.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click a highlighted square | Place a disc |
| 1–3 | AI level |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. A move must flip at least one enemy disc.
2. Pass happens automatically.
3. The player with the most discs at the end wins.

## Tips
- Corners are the strongest squares.
