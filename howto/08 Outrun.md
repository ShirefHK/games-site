# Outrun

## Goal
Race down the road at full speed through hills and curves.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ↑ | Gas |
| ↓ | Reverse |
| ← → | Steer |
| Tab | Turbo |
| W / S | Camera height |

## How to play
1. Hold ↑ to speed up.
2. Steer into the curves to stay on the road.
3. Use Tab for a turbo boost on the straights.

## Tips
- Raise or lower the camera with W / S until the road is easy to read.
