# Racing (Top Down)

## Goal
Beat 4 AI cars around the track by passing every checkpoint.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ↑ | Accelerate |
| ↓ | Brake / reverse |
| ← → | Steer |

## How to play
1. Accelerate and follow the track.
2. Drive through the checkpoints in order.
3. Finish ahead of the AI cars.

## Tips
- Brake before sharp corners instead of steering into the wall.
