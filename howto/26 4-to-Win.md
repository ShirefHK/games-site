# 4-to-Win

## Goal
Drop discs to connect four in a row.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click a column | Drop a disc |
| 1–4 | AI level |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. Click a column to drop your disc.
2. Connect four in any direction.
3. Block the computer's lines.

## Tips
- Take the middle columns.
