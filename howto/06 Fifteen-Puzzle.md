# Fifteen-Puzzle

## Goal
Slide the numbered tiles until they are in order from 1 to 15, with the gap in the last corner.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Slide a tile that touches the empty square |
| R | Shuffle |

## How to play
1. Click any tile that is next to the empty square to slide it in.
2. Rebuild the first row, then the second, and so on.
3. The puzzle is solved when the numbers read 1–15 in order.

## Tips
- Solve rows from the top; leave the last two rows for the end.
