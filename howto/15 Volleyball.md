# Volleyball

## Goal
Two players, one court. Drop the ball on the other side to score.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← ↑ → | Player 1 (red) |
| W A D | Player 2 (green) |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. Move under the ball and jump to hit it over the net.
2. Score when the ball lands on your opponent's side.
3. First to 10 wins.

## Tips
- Hit the ball near its edge to change the direction.
