# Strike5

## Goal
Line puzzle: make lines of five same-coloured balls to clear them before the board fills.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Pick a ball, then click where to move it |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. Click a ball, then an empty square to move it (the path must be free).
2. Line up 5 or more of one colour to clear them.
3. Check the "Next:" preview to see what is coming.

## Tips
- Keep open space; every move adds new balls.
