# Snake

## Goal
Eat food to grow longer without running into yourself.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Steer the snake |

## How to play
1. Steer with the arrow keys; the snake moves on its own.
2. Edges wrap around, so leaving one side brings you in on the other.
3. Eating your own body cuts your tail off at that point.

## Tips
- Plan turns in advance; the longer you get, the less room you have.
