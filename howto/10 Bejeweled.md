# Bejeweled

## Goal
Swap neighbouring gems to make lines of three or more of the same kind.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click gem, then neighbour | Swap the two gems |

## How to play
1. Click a gem, then click a gem next to it.
2. If the swap makes 3 or more in a row, they pop and new gems fall in.
3. Chain reactions score extra.

## Tips
- Look for moves at the bottom first; matches there can cascade.
