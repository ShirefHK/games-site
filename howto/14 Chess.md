# Chess

## Goal
Classic chess against the computer. Checkmate the opposing king.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag | Move a piece (legal moves only) |
| Space | Computer plays the side to move |
| Backspace | Undo |
| R | New game |

## How to play
1. Drag a piece to a legal square.
2. Press Space to let the computer play its move.
3. Backspace takes back your last move.

## Tips
- Develop pieces and castle early.
- Use undo to learn from a mistake.
