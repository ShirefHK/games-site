# Kuhweide

## Goal
Fence the pastures. Every pasture has exactly 6 cells.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click a grid line | Build / remove a fence |
| R | New puzzle |

## How to play
1. Divide the grid into pastures of 6 cells.
2. Each number must match the fences around its cell.
3. Click grid lines.

## Tips
- Count fences around numbered cells first.
