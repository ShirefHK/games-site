# Arkanoid

## Goal
Bounce the ball off your paddle to destroy every brick.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → | Move the paddle |
| R | Restart |

## How to play
1. Slide the paddle under the ball to bounce it back up.
2. Aim the bounce by hitting the ball with different parts of the paddle.
3. Clear all bricks to win; letting the ball fall loses.

## Tips
- Hit the ball near the paddle edge to change its angle.
