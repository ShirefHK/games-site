# Mahjong Solitaire

## Goal
Clear the board by removing pairs of matching free tiles.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click, click | Select two matching tiles |
| Right click | Undo |
| R | New game |

## How to play
1. Bright tiles are free: not covered and with a free left or right side.
2. Click two matching free tiles to remove them.
3. Clear the whole pyramid to win.

## Tips
- Think before you match: some tiles unlock many others.
- You can undo with a right click.
