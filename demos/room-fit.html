Room Fit

The real engine turned 2D architectural drawings into furnished, lit VR scenes — automatically laying out furniture to fit each room's real dimensions. This is that core idea, distilled: a greedy wall-anchored placement algorithm that adapts to room size and skips what doesn't fit instead of overlapping it.
