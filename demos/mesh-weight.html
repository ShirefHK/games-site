Mesh Weight

A production BIM model repeats the same handful of components — bolts, brackets, pipe sections, fixtures — thousands of times. Storing a full copy of each one is what makes models heavy. GPU instancing keeps one shared mesh and a tiny per-instance transform instead. Move the sliders to see why that math wins.

MODEL · illustrative, tuned to the shape of a real fix: PlanRadar's heaviest production model went 3.56 GB → 272.5 MB (−92%) after this rewrite.
